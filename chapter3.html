Chapter 3 — Village of Barovia

Past the gates and the Svalich Woods, the road drops into a hollow where the village of Barovia huddles under the shadow of Castle Ravenloft. Chimneys trickle thin smoke. Shutters stay barred long after dawn. Strangers are noticed — and seldom welcomed.

This is the first true settlement most travelers reach: a place of fear, rumor, and thin hospitality. The Burgomaster’s mansion, the church, and a handful of shops cling to the muddy lanes while the castle watches from the cliffs above.

Mood of the village

Barovians here speak softly and lock early. Many have given up hoping for rescue; others still light a candle for the Morninglord and wait for a miracle that never comes. Adventurers who swagger through the square will find closed doors. Those who offer help — or at least courtesy — may learn who still holds a scrap of courage.

What to explore

Useful stops for a first visit include the inn and tavern for news, the church for local faith and burial customs, and the Burgomaster’s household if the party seeks authority — or trouble. Side lanes hide poorer homes, a cemetery at the edge of town, and paths that climb toward the castle’s approach.

Do not treat the village as a safe hub. Wolves and worse still hunt the outskirts, and Strahd’s attention reaches every rooftop. Leave when you must, return when you dare — but understand that once you have been seen here, the valley already knows your names.
