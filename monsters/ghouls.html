Ghouls

Ghouls stalk graveyards, crypts, and forgotten cellars where the dead are left too long. In Barovia they are drawn to places of despair — churchyards after a plague winter, family tombs under neglected mansions, and roadside graves that never received a proper rite. Their hunger is not only for flesh; it is for the fear that hangs around unfinished mourning.

Barovians bury their dead carefully when they can. When they cannot, something else finishes the work.

Where they wait

Look for disturbed earth, clawed coffin lids, and bones dragged toward deeper dark. A single ghoul may lure the curious; a nest waits for the rescue attempt. Undead in this valley often serve a greater will — clearing a crypt does not always clear the curse that filled it.

Facing them

Do not fight them in a choke point they chose. Radiant force, fire, and refusing to chase the wounded into tunnels keep the party alive. If a ghoul speaks, listen once — then end it. Bargains with the hungry dead rarely outlast the next meal.
