Bats

At dusk the skies above Barovia thicken with wings. Ordinary bats roost in castle ruins, church eaves, and cliff hollows — but swarms that move with unnatural purpose often answer to darker masters. When the air fills with screeching and the lantern gutters, something else is usually watching.

Villagers know the difference between a quiet evening flight and a storm of leather wings that circles a single house. The second kind is a message: you have been noticed.

Signs and omens

Sudden silence in a bat-loud night can be worse than the noise. A swarm that parts around a rider, a cloud that follows the party from town to town, or bats that enter a sealed room are not coincidence. In Ravenloft’s valley, vermin are often scouts.

Facing them

Light and area defenses help more than single-target heroics. Cover faces, protect the wounded, and do not waste the whole fight on the swarm if a greater horror is using it as cover. When the bats break, look for what arrives in their wake — mist, wolves, or a guest who did not knock.
