Vallaki

West of the village of Barovia, behind timber walls and watchful gates, stands Vallaki — the largest town many travelers will see in the valley. Market stalls, inns, and a crowded square give the place a pulse the smaller village lacks. That pulse is forced. Banners and festivals paper over fear; smiling when told to smile is a local survival skill.

Inside the walls, politics matter as much as monsters. Rival households compete for the Burgomaster’s favor, the church offers a thin shelter of faith, and rumors travel faster than carts on the muddy streets. Outsiders who pick a side too quickly may find every door locked the next morning.

Walls and watch

Vallaki’s stockade is both comfort and cage. Guards question strangers, festivals fill the calendar, and leaving town after dark is discouraged — sometimes by policy, sometimes by wolves. The walls keep some horrors out. They also keep secrets in.

What to explore

Useful first stops: the town square and inns for gossip, the church for local faith and sanctuary rumors, and the Burgomaster’s manor if the party seeks papers, lodging, or official trouble. Side streets hide poorer wards, rival salons, and people who will trade truth for coin — or for silence.

Treat Vallaki as a stage, not a sanctuary. Strahd’s reach does not stop at the gate, and a cheerful crowd can turn in a single speech. Listen more than you boast; the town is always listening back.
