Wolves

Wolves haunt the Svalich Woods and the roads that cut through them. Travelers hear them before they see them — a chorus in the mist that seems to move when the fog moves. In Barovia, a wolf pack is rarely only hungry animals. Many serve as eyes and teeth for Strahd, driven to harry the lost and cut off escape.

Barovians shutter early for a reason. Lone riders and small parties on the Old Svalich Road learn quickly that running blindly into the trees is how the valley feeds itself.

How they hunt

Packs prefer to isolate stragglers, force horses to panic, and drive prey toward denser timber or open ground where larger threats wait. Daylight does not make the woods safe; it only makes the silence louder. Adventurers who treat every howl as “just wolves” often discover the truth with blood on the snow.

Facing them

Fire, tight formation, and refusing to scatter buy time. Mercy is rare — a wounded pack may withdraw, but the land remembers scent and fear. If the wolves break off without a kill, ask what they were herding you toward.
