Vistani

The Vistani are a wandering people who travel the Domains of Dread in brightly painted wagons called vardos. Unlike the Barovians trapped in Strahd’s valley, many Vistani can pass through the Mists — a freedom that makes them both valuable guides and objects of deep suspicion.

They are not a single tribe with one voice. Camps differ in custom, fortune-telling craft, and how closely they deal with the Devil Strahd. Some caravans trade openly with villages; others keep to the roads and clearings of the Svalich Woods. What they share is a reputation for reading fate in cards, crystal, and song — and for knowing more about Barovia’s curses than they usually admit.

Fortune and the Tarokka

Vistani seers often use a Tarokka deck to cast fortunes. In Barovia, such readings are never idle entertainment: a true reading can name the heroes’ role in Strahd’s long game, point toward relics of hope, or warn of allies who will betray them. Travelers who scorn a camp’s hospitality may find the roads darker afterward.

Strahd’s arrangement

Strahd allows certain Vistani to come and go where others cannot. That privilege is not kindness. It is leverage — information, messengers, and eyes along the Old Svalich Road. Barovians who have suffered under the night often blame every Vistani for the bargains of a few, so camps near Vallaki or the village of Barovia walk a careful line between outsider and insider.

For adventurers, the lesson is simple: treat a Vistani camp as a people, not a prop. Earn trust, pay fairly, and listen when a seer lays the cards — or leave before the fires die and the wagons roll on into the fog.
